## 3D models

The launch animation's wireframes are made from these models. The originals are in
[`examples/wireframe/models/`](examples/wireframe/models). They were downloaded from
[Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza), and all of them are licensed under
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

The wireframes are changed versions of the originals. `wireframe-import` keeps only some of their
edges, scales them to real size, turns them, and adds the rectangle the game is drawn in.

| Model                                                          | Author                                                      | Wireframe                         |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| [Gameboy](https://poly.pizza/m/dczUnD4aslM)                    | [Jason Toff](https://poly.pizza/u/Jason%20Toff)             | `examples/wireframe/gameboy.json` |
| [NES](https://poly.pizza/m/bEUJNyZrdlp)                        | [Jason Toff](https://poly.pizza/u/Jason%20Toff)             | not made yet                      |
| [SNES](https://poly.pizza/m/7yVvvpp-ftQ)                       | [Gabriel Valdivia](https://poly.pizza/u/Gabriel%20Valdivia) | not made yet                      |
